`data/reference/` contains AME(4,6) states from other publications, reproduced only to verify the comparisons:
- `golden_rbb22_U36.npy`: the golden 2-unitary of S. A. Rather et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 128, 080507 (2022)
  (numerical copy);
- `bz24_H0_B.json`: the matrix B of W. Bruzda and K. Życzkowski, arXiv:2401.01671, Appendix A
  (H(0) = exp(iπB/3)/36).
Please cite those works when using these files.

The manuscript sources in `paper/` are not covered by either licence.
